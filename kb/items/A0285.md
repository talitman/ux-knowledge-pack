## סיכום

- **הפיצ'ר:** ב-ChatGPT אפשר לתזמן **משימות אוטומטיות** (Tasks) לכל לו"ז ולקבל התראה במייל. הם ממליצים לבחור במודל **o4-mini**.
- **השימוש:** מכונת תוכן אוטומטית: חיפוש, סיכום, פוסט ומאמר.

### פרומפטים
```text
I need you to set up a weekly task. Every Monday and Thursday at 9am BST you'll use your web search feature to find the most interesting news stories in the field of AI since the last task run was performed. Here's what you'll do with the results.
1. Provide me with a 100 word summary of the top 3 news articles that I can copy and paste into a newsletter for my subscribers. Each summary should have a source linked to the original article.
2. Create a 200 word thought provoking post for LinkedIn on the news story that is most likely to engage users. It will be written to fit the platform but do not abandon my writing style. Avoid AI cliches and hyperbolic phrases.
3. Write a 500 word SEO optimized article based on the news story. The article should be written in my style using easy to read language and offer an interesting take on the subject.
Important: turn email notifications on and alert me via email every time the task has been completed.
```
