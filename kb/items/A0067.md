### פרומפטים
```text
I want to learn about (INSERT TOPIC). Determine and share the 20% of the topic's lessons that are most crucial to understanding the remaining 80%.
```
```text
I am learning about (INSERT TOPIC) right now. Put my knowledge to the test by asking me a series of questions. Provide me with better answers to close any knowledge gaps in my responses.
```
```text
I want to learn (INSERT DESIRED SKILL). Make a 30-day study schedule that will assist someone like me who is just starting to learn this skill in developing it.
```
```text
Develop a detailed roadmap for mastering (TOPIC). Break it into clearly defined phases, such as foundational learning, intermediate skills, and advanced expertise. Include specific tools, resources, best practices, and timelines for each phase.
```
